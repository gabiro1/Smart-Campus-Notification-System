# Research Questions for the Smart Campus Notification System

1. **How effective is the AI‑based event detection model at accurately identifying campus events from heterogeneous data sources (e.g., calendars, social media, IoT sensors)?**
2. **What is the latency between event occurrence and user notification, and how does it impact user satisfaction?**
3. **How does personalized reminder timing (based on user preferences and context) affect event attendance rates compared to static reminders?**
4. **What privacy and security risks arise from aggregating student data for personalized alerts, and how can differential privacy mitigate them?**
5. **How does the system’s scalability behave under peak campus activity (e.g., exam periods) in terms of response time and throughput?**
6. **What is the impact of AI‑generated paraphrasing of event details on comprehension and perceived relevance among different user groups (students, faculty, staff)?**
7. **How does the integration of role‑based dashboards (Dean, HOD, Principal) influence administrative decision‑making efficiency?**
8. **Can reinforcement learning be employed to optimize the scheduling of reminders to maximize engagement while minimizing notification fatigue?**
9. **What are the comparative advantages of using a micro‑service architecture versus a monolithic design for this campus notification system?**
10. **How does the system perform in low‑connectivity environments common on campuses, and what fallback mechanisms are most effective?**
11. **What usability issues emerge during real‑world deployment, and how can iterative user‑centered design improve the overall experience?**
12. **How does the accuracy of the system’s audit logs affect compliance with institutional policies and regulations?**
13. **What is the cost‑benefit analysis of deploying this system at scale across multiple campuses?**
14. **How does the system handle conflicting event notifications (e.g., overlapping schedules) and what conflict‑resolution strategies are most user‑friendly?**
15. **To what extent does the inclusion of AI‑driven sentiment analysis on event feedback improve future event recommendations?**
